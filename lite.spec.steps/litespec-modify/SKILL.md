---
name: litespec-modify
description: Revise an active LiteSpec change when user decisions or relevant source files change, preserving unaffected context and progress.
---

Read the [shared rules](../litespec-shared/protocol.md).

1. Identify the change and the decision or source that changed. Ask if ambiguous.
2. Follow [incremental revision](../litespec-shared/revision.md).
3. Report revised decisions, affected steps/checks and blockers. Continue through litespec-apply only for authorized, unblocked implementation; a spec-only request stops here.
